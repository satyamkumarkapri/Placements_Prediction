Density-Based Clustering (DBSCAN)

Unlike K-Means, DBSCAN does not force every point into a cluster. It identifies regions of high density and explicitly labels scattered points in sparse regions as "noise". It also determines the number of clusters automatically based on the 'eps' (radius) and 'min_samples' parameters.

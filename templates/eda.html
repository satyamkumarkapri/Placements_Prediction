Key Insights

The Academic Impact (CGPA)

CGPA is the Ultimate Gatekeeper

The boxplot clearly demonstrates that students who secure placements have a significantly higher median CGPA compared to unplaced students. While a high CGPA doesn't guarantee a job on its own, it acts as a strict filtering criteria for top-tier companies. Students in the lower quartile of CGPA struggle significantly regardless of other skills.

Core Skills (Coding & Mock Interviews)

Coding Test Performance

A left-skewed distribution indicates the majority of students score well, making high coding scores a baseline requirement rather than a unique differentiator.

Mock Interview Performance

Mock interview scores represent soft skills and technical communication, which strongly correlate with final HR round clearances.

Salary Outcomes

The LPA Distribution

The regression target in our dataset is the `Salary Package`. The distribution shows a bimodal shape. A large spike at 0 indicates unplaced students. For placed students, the packages cluster tightly around the 8-10 LPA mark, representing the mass-recruiter and standard IT services benchmark, with a long tail extending towards 25+ LPA for premium product roles.

Global Feature Correlations

What Drives Placement Success?

The heatmap maps the Pearson correlation coefficient between all numerical variables.

Look closely at the `PlacementStatus` row. Variables with the darkest blue squares are the strongest positive predictors. In our dataset, academic consistency (CGPA) and practical exposure (Internships) form the bedrock of a hirable profile.

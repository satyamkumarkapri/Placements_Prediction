Inside the AI's Brain (SHAP)

Machine learning models are often considered "black boxes". We use SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations), an advanced game-theoretic approach, to break open the box and explain exactly which features drive our predictions.

Global Feature Importance & Impact

How to read this chart:

  • Y-Axis: Features ordered by their importance to the model (top is most important).
  • X-Axis (SHAP Value): Shows whether the feature pushed the prediction towards "Placed" (Right/Positive) or "Not Placed" (Left/Negative).
  • Color: The actual value of the feature for a student. Red = High value (e.g., High CGPA), Blue = Low value.
  • Insight: Notice how high CGPAs (red dots on the top row) strongly push the prediction to the right (placed), while low CGPAs (blue dots) push it to the left (rejected).

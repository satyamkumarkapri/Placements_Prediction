Data Dictionary

A detailed breakdown of the critical columns in our placement dataset and their relevance to the predictive model.

CGPA & SGPA

Cumulative and Semester-wise Grade Point Averages. High academic consistency is strongly correlated with placement probability.

Internships & Projects

Quantifies practical experience. Usually, 2+ internships significantly boost expected Salary Package (LPA).

CodingTest & MockInterview

Direct skill assessment metrics out of 100. Core drivers for the Random Forest classification model.

PlacementStatus (Target)

The primary binary target variable (0 = Not Placed, 1 = Placed) for our supervised learning algorithms.
